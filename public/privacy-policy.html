Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 23, 2026

LinguDiary Team ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Lingu Diary mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable privacy laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of South Korea.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address: Used for account creation, authentication, and communication
  • Authentication credentials: Managed securely through Supabase

1.2 User-Generated Content

  • English diary entries: The content you write in the app
  • AI-generated feedback: Corrections, tips, and suggestions provided by our AI service
  • Learning statistics: Progress tracking, streaks, vocabulary lists, and achievement data

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information: Non-identifiable device type and operating system version
  • Usage logs: App usage patterns for service improvement
  • Error diagnostics: Crash reports and technical errors (non-personal)

1.4 Subscription Information

  • Payment processing: All payments are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store
  • We do not collect or store: Credit card information, billing addresses, or any payment details
  • Subscription status: We track your subscription tier (Free/Plus) to provide appropriate features

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: Provide AI-powered English correction and feedback
  • Content storage: Save your diary entries and learning progress
  • Feature provision: Track streaks, vocabulary growth, and learning statistics
  • Service improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance the app
  • Customer support: Respond to your inquiries and technical issues
  • Account management: Authenticate users and maintain account security

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

3.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following trusted third-party services:

Database & Authentication

  • Supabase: Stores your account data, diary entries, and learning statistics
  • Purpose: Database hosting and user authentication
  • Data processed: Email, diary content, learning data

AI Processing Services

  • Google Gemini: AI-powered English correction and feedback
  • Purpose: Generate writing corrections and learning tips
  • Data processed: Diary entry text only (transmitted via secure API)

Important Notes:

  • Your diary content is sent to AI services only when you request feedback
  • AI providers process your text solely for correction purposes
  • We do not use your diary content to train AI models
  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

4. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed on servers located outside of South Korea, including:

  • Supabase servers: United States
  • AI processing: May involve servers in the United States or other countries

We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

5.1 Retention Period

  • Active accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Legal obligations: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

5.2 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the app's Settings. Upon deletion:

  • All diary entries are permanently deleted
  • All learning statistics are removed
  • Your email and authentication data are erased
  • This action is irreversible

6. Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Restriction: Request limitation of data processing
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain types of data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: support.lingudiary@gmail.com

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: Data transmission uses HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • Access control: Restricted access to personal data
  • Secure authentication: Password hashing and secure session management
  • Regular monitoring: Continuous security assessments

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Lingu Diary is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Email notifications (if applicable)
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy:

LinguDiary Team
Email: support.lingudiary@gmail.com

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea.
